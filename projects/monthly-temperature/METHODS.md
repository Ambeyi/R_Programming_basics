# Calculation methods

Annual means weight the available monthly observations equally. They are not day-weighted annual means. Annual minimum and maximum refer to monthly values, not daily extremes. Incomplete-year means use available months and are explicitly flagged.

Seasonal summaries pool each calendar month across available years. Standard deviations use a sample denominator of n−1 and are undefined for a single observation. Values retain their input units without conversion.

These descriptive summaries do not perform trend significance testing, establish causes of temperature changes or forecast future conditions. The series is a historical snapshot ending in 2024.
