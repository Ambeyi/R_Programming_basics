# Methods

Seven `imp_` columns capture product importance on an integer 1–7 scale. Blank cells are missing; nonnumeric, nonfinite, noninteger and out-of-range values are invalid. Neither category is imputed. Attribute means and medians use available valid ratings. Distribution counts expose the full ordinal response pattern; means treat adjacent scale steps as equal.

Top-choice counts use only complete responses. Each attribute tied at a respondent's maximum receives one count, so percentages can sum above 100%. The denominator is the number of complete responses, including ties. A zero denominator yields an empty percentage field. Attributes are ordered by descending mean with alphabetical tie breaking.

These descriptive summaries do not estimate purchase likelihood, population preferences or causal effects. No respondent identifiers or demographic fields are needed. The bundled example is synthetic; committed results summarize the supplied 105-response survey.
